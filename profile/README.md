**XNOVEX** is an AI and intelligent systems engineering company. We turn complex data,
signals, and ideas into practical, intelligent technology, combining machine learning,
signal processing, and software engineering to build systems that work in the real world.

### What we build

- **AI-powered solutions:** machine learning, computer vision, forecasting, and AI assistants
- **Custom web apps:** full-stack platforms with Django and Next.js
- **Professional dashboards:** data, analytics, and business intelligence
- **Websites & SEO:** fast, search-ready sites for businesses
